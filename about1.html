About

Tiffany See is a designer, sculptor, and photographer from Singapore who currently lives in Los Angeles. This website is an online archive to share their projects and explorations. As someone heavily influenced by the place they inhabit, they have a curiosity about Los Angeles and Americana as a non-American observer. They are curious about how modern identity is shaped against metropolitan yet historical landscapes, specifically the balance between preserving important historical aspects of a place and making way for new and needed improvements to better the livelihoods of all.
